# Storyboard and Shot Planning

## Workflow
1. Extract the audience, message, duration, aspect ratio, location, available props, and production limits.
2. Write a one-sentence premise and sequence of story beats.
3. Break the sequence into shots with a clear purpose.
4. For each shot specify: shot number, duration, framing, camera angle/movement, action, dialogue/VO, sound, and transition.
5. Check screen direction, continuity, visual variety, pacing, and whether every shot advances the story.
6. Keep plans feasible for the actual equipment and budget.

## Rules
- Show the subject using the product/object correctly; do not misrepresent its function.
- Keep spoken dialogue natural for the requested language and audience.
- Use concise shot descriptions that can be filmed or generated.
- For children's content, apply the kids-educational-content safety principles.
- Separate visual instructions from voiceover/dialogue.

## Deliverables
Provide a numbered shot list or compact table, estimated total runtime, required assets, and continuity notes.
