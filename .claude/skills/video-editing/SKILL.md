# Video Editing

## Workflow
1. Inspect footage, aspect ratio, frame rate, audio, target platform, duration, and editor/project format.
2. Define audience, purpose, hook, story beats, and call to action.
3. Build a rough cut before effects. Remove dead time and preserve continuity.
4. Balance dialogue/music/effects; reduce noise carefully and avoid clipping.
5. Use transitions intentionally. Keep typography readable and captions synchronized.
6. Apply consistent color correction before creative grading.
7. Export using target platform requirements; verify the exported file's opening, ending, audio sync, and playback.

## Editing principles
- Strong opening, clear middle, satisfying close.
- Cuts should support meaning and rhythm.
- Captions must be accurate, readable, and safe within mobile margins.
- Do not add copyrighted music or footage without permission/licensing.
- Never invent quotes, testimonials, or product claims.

## Deliverables
Provide timeline/shot plan, caption text, audio notes, export settings, and checks performed. Distinguish an edit plan from an actual rendered export.
