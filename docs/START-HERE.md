# Start Here — Your First AI Creative Studio Project

Welcome! You do not need to be an expert. Your AI assistant should guide you through each step and explain unfamiliar terms.

## Step 1 — Open the repository with an AI assistant

Choose the coding assistant you already have access to:
- **Codex:** open the repository as your working project.
- **Claude Code:** open a terminal in the repository and start Claude Code.
- **GitHub Copilot:** open the repository in a supported editor with Copilot enabled.

The repository includes agent instructions and skill files. Availability and automatic skill discovery depend on the assistant and its current version.

## Step 2 — Describe your idea

You can write in Malay. Example:

> Aku nak buat game 3D dunia terbuka yang nampak realistik. Aku beginner dan laptop aku tak kuat. Tolong bimbing aku dari kosong. Jangan buat semua benda sekali gus. Mulakan dengan cadangan software percuma dan prototype paling kecil yang boleh aku cuba.

Or:

> Aku nak buat video animasi pendidikan 30 saat untuk budak sekolah rendah. Bahasa Melayu, suara natural, visual menarik. Sediakan skrip dan storyboard dulu.

## Step 3 — Let the assistant prepare a plan

Ask it to create:
1. A short creative brief.
2. A list of tools and assets, including free options.
3. A storyboard or game design outline.
4. A small first milestone.
5. Clear instructions for testing the result.

Do not install large software or download large assets until you understand why they are needed.

## Step 4 — Build one small piece

Examples:
- Game: one small scene, one character, one working movement mechanic.
- Animation: one short shot with a moving character and camera.
- Video: a 10–30 second rough cut.
- Education: one mini-lesson with a simple activity and recap.

## Step 5 — Review and improve

Ask the assistant to check errors, clarity, motion, sound, safety, and performance. Fix the most important problems first.

## Step 6 — Export and keep your work

Keep editable project files. Put final videos/builds in an exports folder. Keep notes about tools, versions, asset sources, and licenses.

## If you get stuck

Copy the exact error message or attach a screenshot and say:

> Aku beginner. Terangkan maksud error ni dalam bahasa mudah, kemudian baiki langkah demi langkah. Jangan padam atau ubah bahagian lain yang masih berfungsi.
