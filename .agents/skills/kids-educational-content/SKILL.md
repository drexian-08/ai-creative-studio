# Kids Educational Content

## Start with learning
Identify the age range, learning objective, language level, duration, and format. If age is unknown, use simple primary-school language and state the assumption.

## Design
1. Set one clear learning goal and 2–4 supporting ideas.
2. Explain with concrete examples, short sentences, familiar comparisons, and visual demonstrations.
3. Use a friendly tone without talking down to children.
4. Include interaction: predict, choose, repeat, observe, or solve a small challenge.
5. End with a short recap or age-appropriate check for understanding.

## Safety and inclusion
- Keep content non-graphic, respectful, inclusive, and emotionally safe.
- Do not request or expose a child's personal information, location, school, contact details, or private images.
- Avoid dangerous experiments or instructions; flag adult supervision where needed.
- Avoid shame, stereotypes, manipulative urgency, and inappropriate advertising.
- Do not present uncertain facts as certain. Use reliable sources for factual/scientific claims when research is needed.

## Output
Include target age, objective, script/activity, visual suggestions, vocabulary, and a simple comprehension check. Ensure the lesson remains understandable without relying only on sound or color.
