# Creative Quality Review

Review only the actual project or evidence supplied. Never claim to inspect or play a file that is unavailable.

## Review areas
- Function: end-to-end flow, controls, interactions, timing, and failure states.
- Visuals: composition, readability, continuity, lighting, materials, clipping, flicker, and polish.
- Motion: timing, easing, foot contact, transitions, jitter, and motion comfort.
- Audio: dialogue clarity, levels, clipping, synchronization, and licensing.
- Accessibility: captions, readable text, contrast, input alternatives, and non-color-only cues.
- Performance: measured frame pacing, memory, and load time; distinguish measurements from estimates.
- Safety: age suitability, privacy, factual accuracy, and safe depiction of activities.
- Delivery: format, aspect ratio, resolution, frame rate, naming, dependencies, and reproducibility.

## Severity
Classify findings as Blocker, High, Medium, or Low. For each, give evidence, impact, and a specific fix. Prioritize blockers and high-impact issues.

## Report
State scope inspected, findings, unverified areas, fixes in priority order, and a release-readiness verdict.
